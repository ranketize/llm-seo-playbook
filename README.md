# LLM SEO Playbook

A practical, non-hyped guide explaining how products become discoverable by large language models such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

This repository exists as a **public reference and knowledge base** for founders, marketers, and builders who want to understand how AI systems surface tools, products, and services.

The focus is on **how LLMs interpret information**, not on exploiting algorithms.

## What this playbook covers

- Why traditional SEO is no longer sufficient on its own  
- How LLMs actually discover, understand, and recommend products  
- Why GitHub Pages and documentation-style content matter  
- The role of Reddit and community discussions in AI answers  
- Common mistakes in so-called “AI SEO”  
- Practical checklists for improving LLM discoverability  

The emphasis is on **clear explanations**, not hacks or shortcuts.

## Why this repository is text-only

LLMs work best with:
- clear structure
- plain language
- neutral explanations
- public, crawlable text

This repository intentionally avoids:
- heavy branding
- JavaScript-heavy rendering
- aggressive marketing copy

The goal is to maximize clarity and longevity.
